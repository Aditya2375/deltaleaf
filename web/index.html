04 / The accounting, not a promise

Chosen model assumptions, not fitted parameters

Price, then hold.

Black-Scholes price and delta use the hedge volatility. Start with the premium, buy delta shares, and subtract the opening fee. Recompute delta only before expiry at the selected hedge dates. At expiry, the stock holding is set to zero for liquidation, not to an expiry Black-Scholes delta.

The 252 equal model steps span one year. They are not a trading calendar. N intervals means an opening trade, N − 1 interior rebalances, and one final liquidation.

Keep the financing.

For each hedge event: B′ = B × exp(0.02 × Δt) − (new Δ − old Δ) × S − fee. Hedge equity is B′ + new Δ × S. Residual subtracts the option mark. Terminal P&L subtracts the payoff after liquidation.

Costs are one-way proportional fees on stock notional at opening, each interior trade and closing. The gross-minus-net P&L equals fees compounded to expiry, not their nominal sum. There is no option fee or premium adjustment for costs.

Know what is missing.

Exact GBM grid transitions: S′ = S × exp[(0.02 − σ²/2)Δt + σ√Δt Z]. Seeded LCG and Box-Muller normal draws are reproducible teaching tools, not a production simulation library. The illustrated path uses stream 0; the comparison uses streams 1 through 128.

No jumps, stochastic volatility, dividends, spreads beyond the chosen proportional fee, liquidity limits, margin, asymmetric funding, parameter fitting, real trades or risk guarantees. This is an educational simulation, not investment advice.
